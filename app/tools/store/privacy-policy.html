Independent Video

Privacy Policy · Effective October 16, 2026 · Made by Better App Mafia

What we collect

Nothing. Independent Video has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your photos, videos, projects or any other information anywhere.

Where your work is kept

Your projects, settings and any crash report are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Media you import stays where it is; the app keeps a link to it, or a copy inside the project when that is needed to edit it. When you export, the result is saved to your phone's Pictures, Movies or Download folder. When you tap Share, Android's share sheet sends the file to the app you choose; what that app does with it is covered by its own policy.

Permissions

Independent Video asks for no runtime permissions. It uses Android's photo picker and file picker, which let you choose specific files without giving the app access to your whole library. It uses the "modify audio settings" permission, granted automatically by Android, only to preview the equalizer on its own audio playback.

Deleting your data

Deleting a project in the app moves it to Trash; emptying Trash or uninstalling the app removes it from your phone. Files you exported to your gallery stay until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
