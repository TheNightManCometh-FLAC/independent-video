No account  ·  No uploads  ·  Stays on your phone

A video editor and a layered photo editor in one Android app, for vlogs, short films, music videos, edits for Reels and YouTube, and album art. There's no account and no sign-in, and the app has no internet access, so your footage, photos and projects stay on your phone.

Video

Cut, color, retime and title your footage. Add music, captions and sound.

  • Multi-track timeline: frame-accurate trims, split, roll and slip, B-roll and picture-in-picture
  • Titles and captions, with SRT import and export
  • Speed from 0.1× to 10×, or speed curves you shape point by point
  • Color grading in layers: curves, levels, HSL, LUTs and built-in looks
  • Beat detection, and visualizers that follow the music
  • 6-band EQ on every track, volume keyframes, fades and ducking
  • Export at 720p, 1080p or 4K, 24 to 60 fps

Photo

Layers, masks, curves and effects. Heal, remove and edit RAW photos.

  • Photo, text, fill and adjustment layers with blend modes, groups and clipping
  • Editable masks: brush, gradients, shapes, text and color ranges
  • Selections: lasso, polygon, magic wand, refine edge
  • Heal, clone and content-aware remove
  • Opens JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIF and RAW (DNG); exports full-resolution JPEG or PNG

Privacy

Independent Video collects nothing: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your work never leaves your phone unless you export or share it yourself. Read the privacy policy.
